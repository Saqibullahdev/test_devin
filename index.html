About the tournament

The Peshawar Premier Cup brings together club sides, university teams and neighbourhood academies from across Khyber Pakhtunkhwa for nine days of competitive eleven-a-side football.

Every match is played on full-size turf under KPFA-registered referees, with live commentary and streaming for the knockout rounds. Entry is open to any team with eighteen registered players aged 16 and above.

  • KPFA-registered match officials for every fixture
  • On-site physiotherapist and ambulance cover
  • Live streamed quarter-finals, semi-finals and final
  • Golden Boot, Golden Glove and Player of the Tournament awards

Entry fee

PKR 15,000 per team, covering match officials, kit numbers and medical cover for all group fixtures.

Squad size

Minimum 14, maximum 20 players. Squads lock 48 hours before the opening whistle.

Deadline

Registration closes 1 March 2027 or once sixteen teams have confirmed — whichever comes first.

Format

Four groups of four. Top two from each group progress to the quarter-finals.

01

Group stage

24 matches across six days. Three points for a win, one for a draw, head-to-head record separates level teams.

02

Knockouts

Quarter-finals and semi-finals are single-leg. Extra time is skipped — level scores go straight to penalties.

03

Final

22 March under floodlights at Qayyum Sports Complex, followed by the presentation of the Premier Cup.
